# Policy brief skill file

**Level:** beginner

## The job

Staff receive a long note and need a one-page brief for a decision meeting. The skill file tells the model who the reader is, how long the brief is, which headings to use, and what it must not invent.

## What the group builds

A skill file in `01-context/skill-file.md`. Later modules add requirements, a check that the brief stays faithful to the note, and a decision on whether the brief is worth keeping.

## Allowed

- A fictional note the group writes in class, about a fictional city service.
- Quotes only from that note.

## Not allowed

- A real policy, a real memo, or a real meeting paper.
- Names of real residents or real staff.
- The model adding facts that are not in the note.

## Synthetic starter

Note title: "Harbourlight Library, Saturday hours trial."
The fictional note says the trial ran for four Saturdays, 86 visits were counted, and the desk logged 14 questions about printing. The brief may use only those figures.
