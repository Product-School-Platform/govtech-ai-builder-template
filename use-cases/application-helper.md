# Application helper

**Level:** intermediate

## The job

People stall on a long application. The helper explains the next question in plain language and lists fields that are still empty. It does not submit the application, and it does not decide whether the application is approved.

## What the group builds

Requirements for which questions the helper may explain, a rubric for a good explanation, and a small prototype that walks one fictional application. Sample rows are in `11-data-and-deploy/data/sample-applications.json`.

## Allowed

- A fictional form the group writes, with fictional applicants.
- Explanations that restate the question. They do not add eligibility rules the form does not contain.

## Not allowed

- Real applications, identity numbers, or uploaded documents.
- A submit button, an approval, or a payment.

## Checkpoint

The person using the helper submits the form themselves, outside this prototype. The helper's last line is "This does not submit your application."
