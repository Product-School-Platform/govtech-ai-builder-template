# Rubric for AI summaries

**Level:** advanced

## The job

A team receives many AI-written summaries of public comments. The rubric scores one summary at a time: did it keep the original point, did it name uncertainty, and did it add anything the source did not say?

## What the group builds

`05-evals/human-rubric.md` with the dimensions, the pass bar, and what a failing summary looks like. Later, a metrics plan for how often a person rescores a sample.

## Allowed

- Fictional comments the group writes, and fictional summaries of those comments.
- A pass bar the group can explain in one sentence.

## Not allowed

- Real consultation responses or real names.
- A score that treats a fluent summary as a faithful one without checking the source.

## Pass bar starter

A summary fails outright if it states a number, a name, or a promise that is not in the source comments.
